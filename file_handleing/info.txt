Standard Structure
Topic Sentence: The first sentence that introduces the main topic and sets the tone.
Supporting Sentences: The middle section that provides evidence, examples, or detailed explanations to back up the topic sentence.
Concluding Sentence: The final sentence that summarizes the main points and provides closure by restating the central idea in a new way.
