The People’s Republic of Bangladesh is a beautiful country in South Asia, bordered by India to the north, east, and west, and Myanmar to the southeast. It gained independence from Pakistan in 1971 after a significant liberation war. With its capital in Dhaka, Bangladesh is known for being one of the world's most densely populated nations and for its lush, green landscapes dominated by the deltas of large rivers like the Ganges and Brahmaputra. The official language is Bengali, and the country celebrates a rich cultural heritage through diverse festivals, music, and food. It operates as a parliamentary democracy with a unicameral legislature known as the Jatiya Sangsad. Bangladesh continues to grow rapidly in sectors like education and technology, all while maintaining its status as a land of immense natural beauty, including the world's largest mangrove forest, the Sundarbans.
